Assalamualaikum. Welcome to Qissa Time.

Most nights at our house end the same way: "One more story, please." We wanted stories that were short enough for a sleepy 5-year-old, true to the Quran and hadith, and gentle enough to end the day with.

**Format:** 4-slide carousel, 4:5.

- 02: A person from the gold story, shown without a face. Text: "People are drawn without faces"

- 04: Close-up of a kawaii animal (the dog or an ant). Text: "Japanese-inspired: little chibi figures and kawaii animals"

**Caption**
```
A few parents have asked about the drawings, so here is how we make them.

Our style is inspired by Japanese picture books: small chibi figures, round kawaii animals, soft colours that feel calm at bedtime.

Two rules we keep in every story:
1. People are drawn without faces.
2. Prophets are never pictured. In the ant story, you see the valley, the ants and the army, but not Prophet Sulayman himself.

We wanted the pictures to help children imagine the story while keeping the respect our families care about.

If you have thoughts on the art, we would love to hear them in the comments.
```

**Thread 4: Behind the scenes, building note**
```
A building note. We made Qissa Time work offline on purpose. Bedtime is often in the car back from a relative's house, or a room with weak wifi, and a story that won't load at 9pm is no fun.

Open it once, add it to your home screen, and the stories are there even with no signal.
```

**Thread 5: Behind the scenes, sources**
```
Before any story goes in, we check it against the Quran or the hadith collections and write the source under the title. If a detail is only our retelling for small children, we try to keep it light and not put words in anyone's mouth.

Parents who know their sources well: we are always happy to be corrected. Kindly tell us.
```
