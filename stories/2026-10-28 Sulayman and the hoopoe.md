# Sulayman and the hoopoe 🐦

<!-- page: birdArmy -->
Long ago, Allah made Prophet Sulayman a great king. Allah taught him something amazing: he could understand the language of birds! His army had people, jinn, and lots and lots of birds.

<!-- page: whereHoopoe -->
One day, Sulayman looked over all the birds, one by one. Then he stopped. "Why do I not see the hoopoe?" he asked. The hoopoe is a little bird with a beautiful crown of feathers on its head. "Is it missing?"

<!-- page: emptyPerch -->
The hoopoe was not there. Sulayman was the leader, and every soldier had a job. So he said the hoopoe would be in trouble, unless it came back with a clear, good reason.

<!-- page: hoopoeBack -->
Not long after, the little hoopoe flew back. And it said something very brave to the great king: "I know something that you do not know! I have come from a land called Saba, with news that is true."

<!-- page: sabaThrone -->
The hoopoe explained: "I found a queen ruling the people there. She has been given lots of everything, and she has a great, big throne. But she and her people bow down to the sun, instead of to Allah!"

<!-- page: hoopoeSky, dua -->
The hoopoe was sad about this. It said: "Why don't they bow to Allah, who brings out everything hidden in the heavens and the earth, and knows what we hide and what we show? Allah! There is no god but Him, the Lord of the Mighty Throne."

<!-- page: fairKing -->
Now, what did Sulayman do? He did not shout. He did not say, "You are making it up!" And he did not just believe it straight away either. He said, "We will see whether you have told the truth, or whether you are one of the liars."

<!-- page: kingsLetter -->
So Sulayman wrote a letter and gave it to the hoopoe. "Take this letter of mine and drop it to them," he said. "Then step back, and see what they answer."

<!-- page: letterFlight -->
And the little hoopoe flew off on its important mission. It had told the truth, and now the truth would be checked.

<!-- page: tinyTruth -->
Isn't it wonderful? A tiny bird, telling the truth to a mighty king, and the king being fair enough to listen and check. And it all happened because that little bird cared that people should worship Allah alone.

## Lesson
Always tell the truth, even when you are a little scared. And when someone tells you something, be fair like Sulayman: listen kindly and check, before deciding who is right.

## Ask the kids
The hoopoe was late, but it told the truth about why. If you were late or made a mistake, how could you tell the truth about it like the hoopoe?
