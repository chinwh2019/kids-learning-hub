# The man who helped a thirsty dog

A long time ago, a man was walking through the desert. The sun was hot, and he was very thirsty. He found a well, climbed down carefully, and drank some cool water. Alhamdulillah, he felt better.

When he came back up, he saw a dog nearby. The dog was panting and licking the dry earth. It looked so thirsty, just like he had been.

The man thought, "This dog is thirsty the way I was thirsty." So he climbed down the well again, filled his shoe with water, held it carefully in his mouth so he could climb, and gave the water to the dog. The dog drank and drank until it was happy.

Allah was so pleased with this kind act that He forgave the man's sins.

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ told us this story so we would remember that kindness to animals, and to every living thing, is kindness that Allah loves.

**Lesson:** Even a small act of kindness, like giving water to a thirsty animal, can mean a lot to Allah.

**Ask the kids:** If you saw a thirsty bird or a hungry cat today, what is one kind thing you could do to help?
