# Prophet Yusuf forgives his brothers 🌙

<!-- page: yusufDream -->
A long time ago there was a boy named Yusuf. His father was Prophet Yaqub, and he loved Yusuf very much. One night Yusuf had a special dream: he saw eleven stars, the sun, and the moon bowing down to him. His father said, "Keep this dream to yourself, my son. Allah has chosen you for something good."

<!-- page: badPlan -->
But Yusuf's older brothers were jealous. "Our father loves Yusuf more than us," they grumbled. So they made a bad plan. They took Yusuf out to play, and then they threw him into a deep, dark well!

<!-- page: fakeTears -->
They went home in the evening, crying fake tears, and told their father, "A wolf ate Yusuf." Their father was very sad, but he said, "Beautiful patience. Allah is the One whose help I seek."

<!-- page: wellBottom -->
Yusuf was all alone at the bottom of the well. But he was not really alone, because Allah was with him.

<!-- page: caravan -->
Some travelers came by, lowered a bucket for water, and found a boy inside! They took Yusuf to the land of Egypt.

<!-- page: grainStores -->
Many years passed. Yusuf grew up to be honest, wise, and kind. He went through many hard times, but he always remembered Allah. In the end, the king of Egypt put Yusuf in charge of all the food in the land.

<!-- page: brothersCome -->
Then a terrible hunger came, and people traveled from far away to get food. Guess who came? Yusuf's brothers! They stood in front of him, but they did not know who he was. Yusuf knew them right away.

<!-- page: iAmYusuf -->
Now Yusuf was strong and powerful. He could have punished them for what they did. But what did he do? He said to them, "Do you remember what you did to Yusuf?" They looked closely and gasped, "Are you... Yusuf?" He said, "I am Yusuf. Allah has been kind to us."

<!-- page: noBlame, dua -->
The brothers were ashamed. "We were wrong," they said. And Yusuf said the most beautiful words: "No blame upon you today. May Allah forgive you, and He is the Most Merciful of the merciful."

<!-- page: togetherAgain -->
Then Yusuf brought his whole family to Egypt, and his father Yaqub hugged him again after all those years. Allah made the dream come true, and the family was together again, because Yusuf chose to forgive.

## Lesson

When someone hurts us and later says sorry, we can be like Prophet Yusuf: forgive them with a big heart, just as we hope Allah forgives us.

## Ask the kids

Has anyone ever done something that made you sad? What could you say to them to forgive them, like Yusuf did?
