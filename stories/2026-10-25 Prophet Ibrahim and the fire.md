# Prophet Ibrahim and the fire 🔥

<!-- page: ibrahimTown -->
A long, long time ago, there lived a young man named Ibrahim. Allah had given him wisdom, even when he was young.

<!-- page: statueHall -->
In Ibrahim's town, the people did something very strange. They carved statues out of stone and wood, and they bowed down to them. They called them their gods.

<!-- page: whatStatues -->
Ibrahim asked his father and his people, "What are these statues that you sit and worship all day?"

They said, "We found our fathers worshipping them, so we do the same."

<!-- page: realLord -->
Ibrahim said, "Then you and your fathers have been making a clear mistake! Your real Lord is the Lord of the heavens and the earth. He is the One who made them."

<!-- page: brokenStatues -->
One day, the people went out of town. Ibrahim went into the place where the statues stood. He broke them all into pieces, all except the biggest one. He left that one standing.

<!-- page: whoDidThis -->
When the people came back, they gasped. "Who did this to our gods?"

Someone said, "We heard a young man talking about them. His name is Ibrahim."

<!-- page: askBigOne -->
So they brought Ibrahim in front of everyone. "Did you do this to our gods, O Ibrahim?"

Ibrahim gave a clever answer. "Why not ask the big one? Ask them, if they can speak!"

<!-- page: wentQuiet -->
The people went quiet. They looked down. They knew statues cannot speak. They cannot help anyone, and they cannot hurt anyone.

Ibrahim said, "Then why do you worship things instead of Allah that cannot help you or harm you at all?"

<!-- page: burnHim -->
But instead of listening, the people became angry. They said, "Burn him! Protect your gods!"

<!-- page: hugeFire -->
They built a huge fire and threw Ibrahim into it. But Ibrahim was not alone. Allah was with him.

<!-- page: beCool, dua -->
Allah said: **"O fire, be cool and safe for Ibrahim."**

<!-- page: fireGarden -->
And the fire obeyed! It did not burn Ibrahim. He was safe. The people tried to harm him, but Allah made them the losers, and He saved Ibrahim.

## Lesson

Ibrahim was brave because he trusted Allah. When we stand up for what is right, even when others don't, Allah is with us.

## Ask the kids

If all your friends were doing something wrong, what could you do to be brave like Ibrahim?
