# Prophet Ayyub the patient 💧

<!-- page: ayyubBlessed -->
A long time ago, there lived a prophet named Ayyub, peace be upon him. Allah had given him many blessings. He had a family he loved, good health, and plenty of everything he needed. And Ayyub always said thank you to Allah.

<!-- page: ayyubSick -->
Then one day, things changed. Ayyub became very, very sick. It was not a sickness that went away in a day or a week. It stayed for a long, long time. His body hurt, he grew tired and weak, and he lost many of the good things he once had.

<!-- page: keptPraying -->
Some people might get angry or give up when something like that happens. But not Ayyub. He did not complain about Allah. He kept remembering Allah, he kept praying, and he waited patiently.

<!-- page: ayyubDua, dua -->
And when he called out to Allah, he did it in the most beautiful and polite way. The Quran tells us his words. He said:

"Indeed, harm has touched me, and You are the Most Merciful of the merciful."

<!-- page: gentleWords -->
Can you hear how gentle that is? He did not shout, "Why me?" He simply told Allah how he felt, and he reminded himself that Allah is the Most Merciful of all.

<!-- page: strikeGround -->
Allah heard Ayyub's prayer. And Allah told him: "Strike the ground with your foot!"

<!-- page: spring -->
Ayyub tapped the ground with his foot, and, subhanAllah, cool, fresh water came flowing out. Allah told him this water was for washing and for drinking. Ayyub washed with it and drank from it, and Allah took away his sickness.

<!-- page: familyBack -->
But Allah gave him even more than that. Allah gave him back his family, and as many more again along with them, as a mercy from Allah and a reminder for everyone who thinks and understands.

<!-- page: excellentServant -->
And then Allah said something very special about Ayyub in the Quran:

"Indeed, We found him patient. What an excellent servant! Indeed, he constantly turned back to Allah."

<!-- page: imagineYou -->
Imagine Allah Himself calling you "an excellent servant"! Ayyub earned that by being patient and always turning to Allah, even when things were hard.

## Lesson
When something hard happens, like being sick, feeling sad, or losing something we love, we stay patient, we keep praying, and we tell Allah how we feel politely, because Allah is the Most Merciful and He hears us.

## Ask the kids
What is something hard that happened to you, and what could you say to Allah, like Prophet Ayyub did, the next time you feel that way?
