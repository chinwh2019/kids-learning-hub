# Prophet Yunus and the big fish 🐋

<!-- page: yunusTown -->
A long time ago, Allah sent a prophet named Yunus to a town full of people. Yunus told them, "Worship only Allah, and be good." But the people would not listen. Day after day, they turned away.

<!-- page: fullShip -->
Yunus became upset with them, and he left the town before Allah told him to go. He walked to the sea and got onto a ship that was very full and very heavy.

<!-- page: lots -->
Out on the sea, the people on the ship decided to draw lots, a bit like picking a name out of a hat, to choose who had to leave the ship. And the name that came out was Yunus! So Yunus went into the water.

<!-- page: bigFish -->
Then Allah sent a great big fish, and the fish swallowed Yunus whole!

<!-- page: darkness -->
Imagine how it felt inside. It was dark inside the fish. It was dark deep under the sea. And it was dark because it was night. Darkness, darkness, darkness. Yunus was all alone.

<!-- page: yunusDua, dua -->
But Yunus knew he was never really alone, because Allah is always with us and always hears us. So, right there in the dark, Yunus called out to Allah:

**"La ilaha illa anta, subhanaka, inni kuntu minaz-zalimin."**
"There is no god but You. Glory be to You. I have truly done wrong."

He was saying sorry to Allah, and remembering how great Allah is.

<!-- page: answered -->
And Allah heard him! Allah says in the Quran: "So We answered him and saved him from his sadness. And that is how We save the believers." Allah also tells us that if Yunus had not been someone who glorified Allah, he would have stayed inside that fish for a very, very long time.

<!-- page: beachPlant -->
So the big fish threw Yunus out onto the beach. He was weak and tired. And Allah made a leafy plant grow over him, to give him shade and help him get strong again.

<!-- page: townBelieves -->
When Yunus was well, Allah sent him back to his people, more than a hundred thousand of them. And guess what? This time, they believed! They turned to Allah, and Allah let them enjoy their lives for a while.

<!-- page: duaToday -->
Our Prophet Muhammad ﷺ taught us that the du'a Yunus said inside the fish is very special: whenever a Muslim says it and asks Allah for something, Allah answers.

## Lesson
Whenever we feel sad, scared, or know we did something wrong, we can always call out to Allah and say sorry. Allah hears us anywhere, even in the darkest place, and He loves to help those who turn to Him.

## Ask the kids
Where was Yunus when he made du'a, and can you learn his special du'a with me: "La ilaha illa anta, subhanaka, inni kuntu minaz-zalimin"?
