# The camel who cried 🐪

<!-- page: palmGarden -->
A long time ago, in the city of Madinah, there were gardens full of tall palm trees. One day, the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ walked into one of these gardens. It belonged to a man from the Ansar, the helpers of Madinah.

<!-- page: sadCamel -->
In the garden there was a camel. A big camel, with long legs and a tall hump.

But this camel was not happy.

<!-- page: camelCries -->
When the camel saw the Prophet ﷺ, it made a sad, sad sound, a long groan, like someone who has been waiting a long time to tell their troubles. And then something amazing happened. Tears began to roll from the camel's big eyes. *Drip... drip...* The camel was crying.

<!-- page: camelSpeaks -->
Have you ever seen an animal cry? The camel was trying to say something, and the Prophet ﷺ understood.

<!-- page: gentleTouch -->
The Prophet ﷺ did not walk away. He did not say, "It's just a camel." He went right up to it, gently, and he stroked the camel softly behind its head and ears, the way you might comfort a friend who is upset.

<!-- page: camelCalm -->
And slowly, slowly, the camel became calm. Its crying stopped. It felt safe.

<!-- page: whoseCamel -->
Then the Prophet ﷺ asked, "Who owns this camel?"

A young man from the Ansar came running. "It is mine, O Messenger of Allah!"

<!-- page: fearAllah, dua -->
The Prophet ﷺ said to him:

**"Do you not fear Allah regarding this animal that Allah has given you? It has complained to me that you keep it hungry and make it work too hard."** (Sunan Abi Dawud 2549)

<!-- page: camelNeeds -->
Imagine that! The camel could not talk in words, but it told the Prophet ﷺ what was wrong. It was hungry. It was tired. It needed someone to take care of it properly.

<!-- page: caredFor -->
The Prophet ﷺ was teaching the young man, and all of us, something very important: animals are not toys or machines. Allah made them, Allah gave them to us, and Allah sees how we treat them. They feel hunger. They feel tired. They feel sad. And they feel happy when someone is kind to them.

<!-- page: kindToAnimals -->
So when we give our cat some water, when we feed the birds a few crumbs, when we are gentle with a little puppy and don't pull its tail, we are following the Prophet ﷺ, who was kind even to a crying camel.

## Lesson
Allah sees how we treat animals, so like the Prophet ﷺ, we should be gentle with them, feed them, and never make them suffer.

## Ask the kids
If you had a pet camel (or a cat, or a bird), what are three things you would do every day to make sure it is happy and cared for?
