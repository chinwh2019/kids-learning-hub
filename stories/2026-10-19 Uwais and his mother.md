# Uwais and his mother 🤲

<!-- page: farYemen -->
A long time ago, our Prophet Muhammad ﷺ told his companion Umar about a man that Umar had never met. The man lived far away in Yemen, and his name was Uwais.

<!-- page: listening -->
The Prophet ﷺ said: "One day, a man named Uwais will come to you with the travellers from Yemen. He once had a skin illness, and Allah healed him, except for one small spot the size of a coin. He has a mother, and he is very good to her. If he asks Allah for something, Allah answers him. So if you can, ask him to pray to Allah to forgive you."

<!-- page: uwaisMother -->
Think about that! Uwais was not a king. He was not rich or famous. But Allah loved him so much, because of how kindly he treated his mother.

<!-- page: caravan -->
Many years later, Umar had become the leader of the Muslims. Every time a group of travellers came from Yemen, Umar would walk up to them and ask, "Is Uwais among you?" Again and again, the answer was no. But Umar never forgot, and he kept asking.

<!-- page: found -->
Then one day, a group arrived, and Umar asked again. And this time, there he was!

<!-- page: meeting -->
"Are you Uwais?" asked Umar.
"Yes," said Uwais.
"Did you have a skin illness, and Allah healed it, except for a spot the size of a coin?"
"Yes."
"Do you have a mother?"
"Yes," said Uwais.

<!-- page: dua -->
Then Umar told him what the Prophet ﷺ had said about him, and asked, "Please, pray to Allah to forgive me." And Uwais made du'a for Umar.

Imagine that: Umar, the great leader of all the Muslims, asking a quiet, humble man to pray for him, all because that man took such good care of his mother.

<!-- page: kufa -->
Umar then asked, "Where are you going?" Uwais said, "To Kufa." Umar offered to write a letter to the governor there so he would be looked after. But Uwais said, "I would rather stay among the ordinary, poor people." He didn't want to be treated as someone special.

## Lesson
Being kind and gentle to our parents is something Allah loves very much. You don't have to be famous or rich to be special to Allah: helping and obeying your mum and dad can make you beloved to Him.

## Ask the kids
What is one kind thing you can do for Mama or Baba tomorrow, without them even asking?
