# The honest men and the jar of gold 🏺

<!-- page: sale -->
Our Prophet Muhammad ﷺ once told his companions a true story about two men who lived long, long ago, before his time.

One man owned a piece of land, and he sold it to another man. <!-- page: digging --> The new owner was happy with his land, and he began to dig in it. Suddenly, his shovel hit something hard. He dug a little more and found a jar. He opened it, and it was full of shining gold!

<!-- page: jarGlow -->
Now, he could have kept it all for himself. Nobody had seen him find it. But he knew Allah sees everything. So he picked up the jar and went straight to the man who sold him the land.

<!-- page: backForth -->
"Here, take your gold," he said. "I only bought the land from you. I didn't buy any gold."

But the other man shook his head. "No, no," he said. "I sold you the land and everything in it. The gold belongs to you."

<!-- page: wiseMan -->
Each man wanted the other to have the gold! Neither of them wanted to take something that might not be his. So they went to a wise man and asked him to decide.

The wise man listened. Then he asked, "Do you have any children?"

The first man said, "I have a son."
The second man said, "I have a daughter."

<!-- page: wedding -->
The wise man smiled. "Then let your son and your daughter marry each other. Spend some of the gold on them, and give the rest in charity to help others."

<!-- page: charity -->
And so the gold that both men were too honest to keep became a blessing for their children and for the poor.

## Lesson
Be honest even when nobody is watching, because Allah always sees us. When we don't take what isn't ours, Allah puts blessing (barakah) in what we have.

## Ask the kids
If you found some money on the floor at school or in the park, and nobody saw you, what would you do? Why?
