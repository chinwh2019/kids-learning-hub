# The guest and the dark lamp 🪔

<!-- page: hungryGuest -->
A long time ago in Madinah, a man came to the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ. He was very, very hungry.

<!-- page: onlyWater -->
The Prophet ﷺ sent a message to his own homes to ask for some food. But the answer came back: "We have nothing except water." The Prophet's own family had no food that day!

<!-- page: whoWill -->
So the Prophet ﷺ turned to his companions and asked, "Who will take this guest home tonight and feed him?"

<!-- page: iWill -->
A man from the Ansar, the helpers of Madinah, said, "I will, O Messenger of Allah!" And he took the hungry guest to his house.

<!-- page: whisper -->
When he got home, he whispered to his wife, "We have a guest of the Prophet ﷺ. Let's look after him well." But his wife whispered back, "We only have enough food for our children."

<!-- page: plan -->
They thought for a moment. Then they made a plan. "Let's gently get the children to sleep," they said. "Then we'll give the food to our guest. And when he sits down to eat, we'll put out the lamp, so it's dark, and we'll pretend we are eating with him."

<!-- page: lampOut -->
So that is what they did. The children were settled to sleep. The food was put in front of the guest. Then the wife got up as if to fix the lamp, and, oops, the lamp went out! In the dark, the husband and wife moved their hands and mouths as if they were eating too, so the guest would feel comfortable and eat as much as he wanted.

<!-- page: happyHearts -->
The guest ate until he was full. And the kind husband and wife went to bed hungry that night, with happy hearts.

<!-- page: verse, dua -->
The next morning, the man went to the Prophet ﷺ. The Prophet ﷺ told him that Allah was pleased with what he and his wife had done for their guest last night. Nobody had told the Prophet about it! Allah Himself sent down a verse in the Quran about people like them:

"...and they give others preference over themselves, even if they are in need." (Quran 59:9)

<!-- page: allahSees -->
Imagine that: a small dinner in a dark room, and Allah sent down a verse about it in the Quran that we still read today!

## Lesson

When we share what we have, even when it's not much, Allah sees it and loves it, even if no one else knows.

## Ask the kids

If you had only one cookie left and your friend was hungry, what would you do? Why?
