# The rain that woke the earth 🌧️

<!-- page: thirstyField -->
Once upon a time, there was a field that was very, very thirsty.

It hadn't rained for a long time. The ground was hard and cracked, like a dry cookie. The grass had turned brown and crunchy.

<!-- page: sleepyFlowers -->
The little flowers had closed their petals and gone to sleep. Even the worms had wriggled deep down, looking for a cool, wet place to hide.

<!-- page: noWaterHere -->
The birds flew over the field and said, "Tweet, tweet, no water here." The bunny hopped by and sniffed. "No fresh green leaves today," she sighed.

<!-- page: windBlows -->
Then one afternoon, something changed. The wind began to blow, *whoooosh*. Far away, big grey clouds started to gather, puffy and heavy, like giant sponges full of water.

<!-- page: cloudsSent -->
Who was sending them? Allah was. He tells the winds where to go, and He drives the clouds across the sky.

<!-- page: firstDrops -->
*Drip.* One drop fell on a dry leaf. *Drip, drip.* Two more landed on a rock.

<!-- page: rainNight -->
And then, *pitter-patter, pitter-patter*, the rain came down, soft and steady, all night long.

<!-- page: groundDrinks -->
The thirsty ground drank and drank. *Gulp, gulp!* The cracks filled up. The worms came wiggling back up, happy.

<!-- page: seedsWhisper -->
The sleepy seeds hidden in the soil felt the cool water and whispered, "Is it time? Is it time to wake up?"

<!-- page: greenShoots -->
And when the sun came out in the morning, guess what? Tiny green shoots were poking out of the ground! Little by little, day by day, the brown field turned green again.

<!-- page: fieldBlooms -->
The flowers opened up: yellow, pink, and purple. The bees came buzzing. The birds splashed in puddles. The bunny found lots of yummy leaves to munch.

The field that looked so tired and empty was full of life again.

<!-- page: mercyVerse -->
Allah tells us about this in the Quran:

**"So observe the effects of the mercy of Allah — how He gives life to the earth after its lifelessness."** (Surah Ar-Rum, 30:50)

<!-- page: arRahman -->
The rain is a gift of Allah's **mercy**. It reminds us of one of His beautiful names: **Ar-Rahman**, the Most Merciful. He takes care of the dry ground, the little seeds, the birds, the bunnies, and the bees. And He takes care of us too: the water we drink, the food we eat, and the hugs we share.

<!-- page: rainDua, dua -->
When the Prophet ﷺ saw rain, he would make a little du'a:

**"Allahumma sayyiban nafi'a."** *"O Allah, make it a beneficial rain."* (Sahih al-Bukhari 1032)

<!-- page: windowRain -->
So next time you hear *pitter-patter* on the window, you can say it too, and smile, because Allah's mercy is falling all around you.

## Lesson

Every drop of rain is a gift from Allah, Ar-Rahman, the Most Merciful, who takes care of everything He made, so we thank Him for His mercy.

## Ask the kids

What is one thing Allah's mercy gave you today, like rain, food, or your family, that you want to say "Alhamdulillah" for?
