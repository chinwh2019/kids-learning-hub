# Young Anas and the errand

<!-- page: anasArrives -->
A long time ago in Madinah, there lived a boy named Anas. When Anas was still small, his mother brought him to the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ so he could help him at home. So Anas grew up close to the Prophet ﷺ, running little jobs for him every day.

<!-- page: errandAsk -->
One day the Prophet ﷺ asked Anas to go and do an errand for him.

<!-- page: wontGo -->
Now, Anas was a boy just like you. And he said, "I won't go!" But inside his heart, he really did plan to go, because the Prophet ﷺ had asked him.

<!-- page: marketBoys -->
So off he went. But on the way, he passed through the market, and there were some boys playing. Play! Fun! Anas stopped to watch them... and maybe he forgot about the errand for a little while.

<!-- page: neckHold -->
Then suddenly, someone gently held the back of his neck from behind. Anas turned around. Who was it? It was the Prophet ﷺ himself, and he was laughing!

<!-- page: unais, dua -->
Was he angry? No. Did he shout? No. He smiled and said kindly, "Unais, did you go where I told you to go?" (Unais was his sweet nickname for Anas, like saying "little Anas.")

<!-- page: offHeGoes -->
And Anas said, "Yes, I am going, O Messenger of Allah!" And this time, he really went.

<!-- page: tenYears -->
When Anas grew up, he told people something amazing. He said, "I served the Prophet ﷺ for ten years, and he never once said 'uff' to me. He never said, 'Why did you do that?' and he never said, 'Why didn't you do that?'"

<!-- page: alwaysGentle -->
Ten whole years, and always gentle. That is how kind our Prophet ﷺ was, even to a little boy who sometimes got distracted.

## Lesson
When someone asks us for help, we should keep our word and do it, and when others make little mistakes, we can be gentle like the Prophet ﷺ instead of getting angry.

## Ask the kids
Have you ever forgotten to do something you were asked? How would you like someone to remind you: with shouting, or with a smile like the Prophet ﷺ?
