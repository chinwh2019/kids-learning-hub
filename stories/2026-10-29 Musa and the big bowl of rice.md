# Musa and the big bowl of rice 🍚

<!-- page: grandmaDinner -->
It was Friday night, and Grandma had come to visit. She made her famous chicken and rice, and she put it in one great big bowl in the middle of the table. It smelled SO good.

<!-- page: grabbyHand -->
Little Musa was very hungry. Before anyone else had even sat down, his hand shot out. He grabbed a piece from this side of the bowl, then a piece from that side, then the biggest piece of chicken from all the way over on the far side, right in front of his sister Aisha.

<!-- page: cheeksFull -->
"Hey!" said Aisha. "That was my side!"

Musa stopped, with his cheeks full like a hamster. Everyone was looking at him.

<!-- page: grandmaPats -->
Grandma did not get cross. She smiled and patted the chair next to her. "Come, habibi. Let me tell you something true, about a boy just like you."

Musa sat down.

<!-- page: aroundDish -->
"A long time ago," said Grandma, "there was a little boy named Umar who lived in the house of our Prophet Muhammad, peace be upon him. One day they were eating together, and little Umar's hand was going all around the dish, here, there, and everywhere, just like yours!"

Musa giggled and looked down at his hand.

<!-- page: oBoy, dua -->
"And do you know what the Prophet said to him? He did not shout. He said gently: 'O boy, say the name of Allah, eat with your right hand, and eat from what is in front of you.'"

<!-- page: countThree -->
Musa thought about it. "Three things," he said, counting on his fingers. "Say bismillah. Use my right hand. Eat from my side."

<!-- page: giftFood -->
"That's it!" said Grandma. "When we say bismillah, we remember that this food is a gift from Allah. And when we eat from our own side, we leave plenty for everyone else."

<!-- page: putBack -->
Musa looked at the big piece of chicken on his plate. Then he looked at Aisha. Slowly, he put it back in front of her. "Sorry, Aisha. That was your side."

Aisha smiled. "You can have half," she said.

<!-- page: familyBismillah -->
Then the whole family put their right hands out together, and Musa said it the loudest of all: "Bismillah!"

And Grandma said it was the best dinner she had ever had.

## Lesson
Before we eat, we say "Bismillah," we eat with our right hand, and we take from the food in front of us, so there is enough for everyone and we remember that the food comes from Allah.

## Ask the kids
What are the three things the Prophet taught little Umar to do when eating? Can you show me at dinner tomorrow?
