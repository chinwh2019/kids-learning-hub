# The three men and the angel 🐑

<!-- page: threeMen -->
Long ago, among the Children of Israel, there were three men. One had a sickness that made his skin sore and patchy. One had no hair at all, and people teased him. And one was blind and could not see anything.

<!-- page: angelSent -->
Allah wanted to test them, so He sent an angel to each of them.

<!-- page: skinHealed -->
The angel came to the first man and asked, "What would you love most?" He said, "Good skin and a healthy color, so people stop staying away from me." The angel wiped over him, and his skin became healthy and beautiful. Then the angel gave him a she-camel that was going to have a baby. "May Allah bless it for you," the angel said.

<!-- page: hairGrows -->
The angel went to the second man and asked the same question. "Nice hair," he said, "so people stop making fun of me." The angel wiped over his head, and beautiful hair grew. The angel gave him a cow that was going to have a baby.

<!-- page: sightBack -->
Then the angel went to the blind man. "What would you love most?" The man said softly, "That Allah gives me back my sight, so I can see people." The angel wiped over his eyes, and he could see! The angel gave him a sheep that was going to have a baby.

<!-- page: valleys -->
The years went by. The camel had babies, the cow had babies, the sheep had babies... until the first man had a whole valley full of camels, the second a valley full of cows, and the third a valley full of sheep. They were all rich now.

<!-- page: angelReturns -->
Then the angel came back. But this time he looked like a poor, tired traveler, just like each man used to look.

<!-- page: firstRefuses -->
He went to the first man and said, "I am poor and far from home. I ask you, by the One who gave you good skin and all this wealth, please give me one camel to help me on my journey." But the man said, "I have too many things to pay for." The angel said, "Don't I know you? Weren't you the sick man people stayed away from, and then Allah gave you all this?" The man said, "No! I got all this from my family." The angel said, "If you are lying, may Allah make you as you were before."

<!-- page: secondRefuses -->
The angel asked the second man the same way, and he said the same thing, so the angel said the same words to him.

<!-- page: sharing -->
Then the angel went to the man who used to be blind. "I am a poor traveler," he said. "Please help me, by the One who gave you back your sight." The man said, "I was blind, and Allah gave me back my sight. I was poor, and Allah made me rich. Take whatever you want! By Allah, I won't stop you from taking anything for the sake of Allah."

<!-- page: pleased -->
The angel smiled and said, "Keep your wealth. You three were only being tested. Allah is pleased with you, and He is displeased with your two companions."

## Lesson

Everything good we have comes from Allah. We show we are thankful by remembering where it came from and by sharing it with others.

## Ask the kids

What is one thing Allah has given you that you could share with someone else this week?
